# Symmetry Breaking

### An Exhibition in Light & Pigment

*A small interactive exploration of physics, light, and artistic expression.*

---

## About

**Symmetry Breaking** is a self-contained digital exhibition exploring the intersection of physical laws and visual art.

Through five interactive installations, the project brings together celestial mechanics, charged-particle dynamics, quantum electrodynamics, and quantum correlations.

Each installation offers a different way to experience the relationship between mathematical structure and artistic expression.

## The Exhibition

**I. Symmetry Breaking**  
A Keplerian orbit responds to external perturbations, revealing how a small influence can reshape an otherwise predictable trajectory.

**II. Bubble Chamber Atelier**  
Charged particles trace delicate paths through a magnetic field, transforming simplified particle dynamics into generative artwork.

**III. Interaction Vertex**  
A visual interpretation of a Feynman diagram explores interaction and the exchange of energy and momentum.

**IV. Non-Local Correlation**  
An interactive spin-singlet simulation illustrates quantum correlations and the relationship \(E(\theta)=-\cos\theta\).

**V. Measurement & Record**  
A quiet closing installation exploring observation, light, and the act of leaving a record.

## Technical Details

- Built with HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript.
- Canvas and SVG-based visualizations.
- Responsive design for desktop and mobile devices.
- No frameworks, external libraries, or third-party assets.
- Runs entirely in the browser.
- No accounts, data collection, or server-side processing.

The physics visualizations use simplified models intended for interactive exploration and artistic interpretation.

## Running Locally

Clone the repository and open `index.html` in a modern web browser.

No installation, build tools, or server configuration is required.
